Why these four.

They are the trades where the same three or four things decide whether the site works, and where guessing wrong costs you real bookings.

The proof is visual

Detailing, landscaping and salon work are all bought off photographs. A gallery is not decoration on those sites, it is the sales page, which is why every one of these builds has one.

The next step is a booking or a quote

A salon needs a slot booked. A landscaper needs enough detail to price the job. Those are different buttons doing different work, and each build carries the right one.

The content changes

A restaurant menu moves more than any other page on any other site here, which is why the restaurant build is the one that comes with a menu you can edit yourself.
